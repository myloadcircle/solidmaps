solidmaps — Privacy

Last updated: 25 October 2026 · solidmaps is operated by Loadcircle Ltd (England and Wales).

What we store

If you create an account, we store the email address you enter. That is the only account data we keep. Vehicle profiles you add (type, dimensions, fuel, emissions class) are stored against your account so your routes use them.

What passes through our servers

Route planning sends your start and destination points to our edge API, which calls open routing and geocoding services (OpenStreetMap Nominatim, OSRM, Overpass API) on your behalf. Queries are cached briefly to keep the service fast; we do not build user profiles from them. During live navigation your GPS position is processed in your browser — it is not uploaded to us.

What we do not do

No advertising trackers, no analytics scripts, no third-party cookies, no selling of data. Your sign-in token lives in your browser's local storage and is sent only to the solidmaps API.

Your data

Email us via loadcircle.co.uk to have your account and vehicle profiles deleted.
